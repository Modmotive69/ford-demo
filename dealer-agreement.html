This Dealer License & Platform Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of ___________________________ (the "Effective Date") by and between PartSites LLC, a limited liability company ("Company"), and the undersigned Ford or Lincoln franchise dealer ("Dealer").

1. Definitions

"FordEngage" means Company's Engage360 platform, branded for Ford and Lincoln franchise dealers, including the Instore Solution, eStore, VDP Widget, DMS integration, and reporting tools made available under this Agreement.

"Dealer Data" means all data collected, stored, or processed by FordEngage on behalf of Dealer, including inventory, transactions, and customer activity specific to Dealer's operations.

"Users" means individuals authorized by Dealer to access FordEngage.

"Terms and Conditions" means the platform terms published at fordengage.com/terms, incorporated herein by reference and updated from time to time.

2. License Grant

2.1 Platform Access

Company grants Dealer and its Users a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access and use FordEngage during the Term solely for Dealer's internal accessory sales operations.

2.2 Dealer Data

Dealer grants Company a limited license to process Dealer Data solely to deliver FordEngage to Dealer. Company shall not sell, share, or use Dealer Data outside of service delivery. Upon termination, Company will return all Dealer Data within ninety (90) days at no charge.

3. Acceptable Use

Dealer agrees not to:

  1. Resell, sublicense, or permit unauthorized access to FordEngage;
  2. Reverse engineer, copy, or create derivative works from FordEngage;
  3. Use FordEngage in violation of applicable law or third-party rights;
  4. Interfere with platform performance or other dealers' access;
  5. Use FordEngage for any purpose other than automotive accessory sales.

4. Advanced Program Transition

Automatic Upgrade to FordEngage

Dealers currently enrolled in the Advanced program (previously offered under the DSS brand) will be automatically upgraded to FordEngage no later than December 31, 2026. The Advanced program will be sunset on that date.

This Agreement governs Dealer's use of FordEngage upon transition. No additional action is required. All Dealer Data will be migrated automatically with no interruption to service. Company will provide no less than 60 days' written notice prior to the transition date.

Dealers who do not wish to continue on FordEngage must notify Company in writing by November 30, 2026. Continued use after December 31, 2026 constitutes acceptance of this Agreement.

5. Terms and Conditions

Dealer's use of FordEngage is governed by the Terms and Conditions at fordengage.com/terms, which cover platform uptime, support, and Dealer responsibilities. Company may update the Terms and Conditions with 30 days' notice. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

6. Term and Termination

6.1 Initial Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months, renewing automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives thirty (30) days' written notice of non-renewal.

6.2 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party materially breaches any term and fails to cure within that period.

6.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination, Dealer's access to FordEngage ceases. Company will deliver Dealer Data to Dealer within ninety (90) days at no charge.

7. Fees

Program fees are set forth in the applicable order form or activation agreement. Company may adjust fees with sixty (60) days' notice prior to any renewal term. All fees are non-refundable except as expressly stated herein.

8. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's non-public confidential information using reasonable care and will not disclose it to third parties without written consent, except as required by law. This obligation survives termination for three (3) years.
